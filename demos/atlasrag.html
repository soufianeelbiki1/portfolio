Evidence contract

The local retriever ranks lexical overlap, the query service filters evidence below a minimum score of 0.25, and the extractive generator only concatenates retrieved evidence.

Answerable-grounding and safe-abstention rates reveal opposite decision failures. Duplicate citations cannot inflate recall; zero false abstentions and zero unsafe evidence responses were observed in this four-case sample.

Current boundary

The metrics are application regression metrics, not model-based semantic groundedness scores. The current report also does not claim measured semantic/vector retrieval quality.

Dataset provenance: hand-authored synthetic engineering facts; no proprietary documents, user data or production query logs.
