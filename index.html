01 · Payments flagship

AtlasPay

Payments · Backend · Distributed systems · PostgreSQL

Implemented

  • Strict ISO 8583 message-body codecs with primary/secondary bitmaps and LLVAR/LLLVAR validation.
  • EMV DE55 BER-TLV parsing, canonical authorization model, STAN/RRN correlation, routing, timeouts, late responses, duplicates, and reversal correlation.
  • Durable PostgreSQL idempotency, append-only balanced double-entry ledger, transactional outbox, idempotent consumers, reconciliation, and bounded replay.
  • Low-cardinality observability and deterministic network fault injection.
  • Scoped ISO 8583 → canonical → ISO 20022 projection with explicit loss boundaries.

Why it matters

The project models payment ambiguity rather than hiding it: a local timeout is not proof of remote failure, at-least-once delivery is not called exactly-once, and accounting invariants are enforced durably.

Analytics layer

CI-tested PostgreSQL marts cover payment cohorts, operation timing, outbox reliability, and daily ledger controls with currency and grain limitations documented.

Truth boundary

AtlasPay is an engineering simulation. It does not process real money, connect to live card networks, or claim production scale or scheme certification.

Principles

What the portfolio optimizes for.

Define the grain. Money, attempts, inventory, assignments, and events are kept at the correct analytical level.

Fail closed. Unsupported mappings, malformed live responses, and unavailable telemetry do not become plausible fake values.

Test behavior and data contracts. CI, warehouse regressions, integration tests, and evaluation datasets matter more than screenshots.

State guarantees precisely. At-least-once is not exactly-once; synthetic findings are not production findings; policy accuracy is not real-world model accuracy.

Use statistics for decisions. Confidence intervals, SRM, CUPED, bootstrap uncertainty, power, calibration and expected cost are tied to explicit decisions.

Document limitations. Every flagship explains what the available data or implementation cannot yet support.
