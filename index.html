Engineering habits

Evidence over decoration.

Failure behavior first

Timeouts, duplicates, late responses, unavailable upstreams and poison/backlog states are modeled instead of only showing happy paths.

Automated checks

Tests, type checks, production builds, container smoke tests and dependency/security checks are used where they materially prove behavior.

Truthful boundaries

Synthetic data, simulations, reference implementations and unmeasured real-world accuracy are clearly separated from production claims.
